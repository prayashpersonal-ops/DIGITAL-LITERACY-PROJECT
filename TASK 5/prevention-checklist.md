

## # Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist

## ## ✅ Do’s

- Always verify the sender before making any online payment

- Check URLs carefully before entering personal or banking details

- Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on important accounts

- Use strong and unique passwords for different platforms

- Keep your apps and devices updated regularly

- Immediately report suspicious activity to cybercrime authorities

- Be cautious while using public Wi-Fi for financial transactions

- Regularly check bank statements and transaction history

##  UPI / Financial Safety (Important)

- Never scan a QR code to receive money — it is only used to pay

- Never share OTP, UPI PIN, or bank details with anyone

## ## ❌ Don’ts

- Do not click on unknown or suspicious links

- Do not trust calls claiming to be from banks without verification

- Avoid sharing personal information on social media

- Do not download apps from untrusted sources

- Do not ignore small suspicious transactions
