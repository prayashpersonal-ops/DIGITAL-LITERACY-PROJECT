

## UPI Payment Fraud – Case Study

UPI payment fraud is a common cybercrime in India where attackers trick users into transferring
money through deceptive methods. This type of fraud often targets students, small business
owners, and individuals who frequently use digital payment apps.

A typical scam begins when the victim receives a call, message, or QR code from a fraudster
pretending to be a buyer, seller, or bank representative. The attacker may claim that they need to
“send money” and ask the victim to scan a QR code or approve a payment request. In reality,
scanning the QR code or approving the request results in money being deducted from the victim’s
account. In some cases, attackers also send fake links that resemble official banking websites to
steal login credentials and OTPs.

The consequences of such fraud include immediate financial loss, emotional stress, and
sometimes loss of trust in digital platforms. Victims may also face difficulty recovering their money
if the fraud is not reported quickly.

To prevent such incidents, users must understand that receiving money never requires scanning a
QR code or sharing an OTP. Awareness and cautious behavior are key to avoiding UPI-related
cybercrimes.
